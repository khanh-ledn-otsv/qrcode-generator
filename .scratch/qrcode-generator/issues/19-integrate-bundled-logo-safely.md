# 19 — Integrate the bundled gray placeholder logo safely

**What to build:** Allow the repository-owned solid-gray placeholder logo only when ECC, knockout geometry, and function-module protection make its placement valid and independently decodable.

**Blocked by:** 17 — Add approved color, contrast, and transparency behavior.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Only `assets/logo-placeholder.svg` is bundled; it is sanitized at build/test time, embedded without runtime requests, and exposed by no upload or arbitrary-SVG path.
- [ ] Enabling the logo switches to ECC H before version fitting and recalculates capacity and diagnostics.
- [ ] The centered logo plus one-module knockout padding stays within the approved matrix-width limit.
- [ ] Any intersection with a finder, separator, timing, alignment, format, version, or fixed-dark module is invalid and disables logo export.
- [ ] Overlapped data and remainder modules are counted and reported, and valid logo mode remains classified as a caution.
- [ ] Transparent QR backgrounds retain an opaque white knockout, and unsafe geometry never forces a larger version solely to create logo space.
- [ ] Every H-level version allowed by each profile either independently decodes with the logo or produces an intentional, tested geometry rejection.
- [ ] Documentation states that replacing the placeholder requires recorded asset license/provenance plus the complete logo validation suite before release.
